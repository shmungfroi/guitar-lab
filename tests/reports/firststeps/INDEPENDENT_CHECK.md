## F1. Finding for Justin: the drawing is only framed, never redrawn

The builder turns and crops your approved plates in steps 2–6. I checked whether that changes the drawing.

- Every plate on screen (steps 1–6) is exactly what `guitarArt()` returns: the SVG markup matches a fresh call character for character (the only difference is the drawing's own id counter, `ga1`, `ga2`…), its `viewBox` is still `0 0 560 832`, and the SVG itself carries no transform and no inline style.
- The app's stylesheet only sets the SVG's display size and margin. It has no rule that reaches a part, a string, a label or a highlight.
- All turning and cropping is on wrapper boxes around the plate:
  - Step 2 (sitting): whole acoustic plate rotated 83°, with the builder's own notes ("strumming hand", "fretting hand", "your right leg", "neck slightly up") drawn in a separate layer on top.
  - Steps 3 and 4: a square window onto the strings between the sound hole and the bridge.
  - Step 5: a window onto the head and the top of the neck, beside the A chord diagram.
  - Step 6: whole acoustic plate rotated 90° so the head is on the left and string 1 is on top, as TAB is written.
- No plate is stretched: every rendered width/height is 0.672–0.673 (560/832 = 0.6731).

So the drawing is unaltered and only framed. What you have not yet approved is the framing itself: the 83° sitting view with the builder's overlay notes, and the three crops. They look right to me (head to the viewer's right, string 6 on top, pickguard below the sound hole, as you see a right-handed player from the front), but they are new compositions made from your art, so they are yours to accept. Screenshots: `check/shots/step2_*.png` to `step6_*.png`.

Two things the approved plate itself brings in, for your eye: the electric plate's label 12 reads "Saddles" where the word list says "Saddle", and it labels two parts that are not booklet terms (14 Controls, 15 Output jack). The builder lists those two as names you can tap but keeps them out of the check.

## New findings

### Should

**S1. A returning 0.9 student can be sent back to First Steps and lose their tabs.**
A save from before 0.11 is recognised each time by "has any progress". If that progress goes away, so does the recognition. Measured: save `{"lv":{"a-e:0":1}}` (case b), lower that one level back to "Not yet": tabs drop to First Steps + Path at once, and after a reload the student is locked in First Steps with every other tab gone. The spec says nobody loses access to anything they already use.
Fix: the first time a pre-0.11 save with progress is loaded, write `fs = {done:[], complete:true, placed:false, all:true}` on the next ordinary save (not at start-up, so the 0.9 rule "never rewrite a save at start-up" still holds). Add a test: one level, lower it, reload, all 7 tabs.

**S2. A new bass, drums, keys or vocals student who taps "Guitar" is locked into guitar First Steps with no way back.**
Measured: `{"inst":"bass"}`, tap Guitar in the picker: view becomes First Steps, one tab, and the instrument picker is hidden (0 picker buttons visible). The only ways out are Teacher mode, passing the placement quiz or finishing all seven steps.
Fix: on First Steps, when the student arrived from another instrument (or simply always), show one quiet line under step 1: "Not learning guitar? Choose your instrument", which opens the picker. Add a test.

**S3. Some First Steps targets are under 44 px.**
Measured at 360 (same at 1280): progress dots 40 × 44; the three honour ticks (steps 2, 4, 7) 41 px high; the memory-line field 41 px high; step 3 number answers "5", "4", "3", "2" 40–42 px wide. The step 1 check itself is fine.
Fix: `min-width:44px; min-height:44px` on `.fst-dots button` and `.fst-opts button`; `min-height:44px` on `.chk` labels and `.fst-line`. Re-measure the dots row at 360 afterwards: it is close to the full width, so it may need a smaller gap or to wrap.

**S4. The test suite is flaky under load, from two causes in the tests.**
- *Cause A, lost seed (8 of the 9 failures).* `tests/uihelp.py open_app` seeds a save by opening the app, writing `localStorage`, hopping to `about:blank`, then opening the app again. When the machine is busy the second load sometimes sees no save, so the test gets a first-visit page ("Meet the guitar", `v-first-steps`, disabled dots, empty "Up next"). Reproduced outside pytest: 4 processes at once, seed lost 7 of 600 loads with the hop and 7 of 600 without it; 10 of 800 with call logging, where the second load's first `getItem` returned `null`. The same seeding against a bare page with no app lost 0 of 800. Seeding with an init script on the same load lost 0 of 600. One process with 8 busy loops lost 0 of 360, which is why quiet runs pass. The difference between the app and the bare page is the app's start-up storage probe (`canStore` writes `{}` to `shredder` and removes it when there is no save, unchanged since 0.9); the seed written straight after it to the same key is the one that goes missing. That points at Chromium's handling of quick successive writes to one key when the page is replaced, but I did not prove the browser internals.
  Fix (tests only): seed with `context.add_init_script` guarded by a `sessionStorage` flag, so the save is in place before the app's script runs and is not rewritten on later reloads in the same tab. That also removes two page loads per test.
- *Cause B, pitch estimate (1 of the 9).* `test_F2_F3_F8_tab_quiz_and_playback` estimated the low E of the E minor stack at 61.85 Hz instead of 82.41 Hz (exactly 3/4). The plucked-string sample is built from random noise each time, and the test's autocorrelation picks a wrong lag now and then. The app's pitch is set by the delay length and is right.
  Fix (tests only): search only lags within a semitone or so of the expected note, or take the shortest lag whose correlation is within 0.05 of the best.
- Real-use risk from cause A looks low (it needs a save written within a fraction of a second of a first-ever load and the page replaced straight after), but it was not tested on a real iPad or Chromebook. If you want belt and braces, the probe could use `try{localStorage.getItem(...)}` plus the first real save's result instead of a write-and-remove.

### Could

- **C1. Failing the placement quiz ticks off steps 2, 4 and 7 as well.** One wrong part question leaves `done = [2,3,4,5,6,7]`: posture, hearing the strings and the first chord with the band are skipped without being checked. Reasonable for someone who nearly passed, but you may prefer step 7 (play Em with the band) to stay open for everyone who does not pass outright.
- **C2. The sitting figure does not say whose view it is.** The words say "seen from the front" only in the screen-reader label. A student may read it as their own view looking down. Add a short caption: "How you look from the front."
- **C3. Blank space around the turned plates.** At 360 the step 6 lying-down guitar has about 100 px of empty space above and below, and step 2 a little. Tighten the box (`aspect-ratio`) to the turned drawing.
- **C4. Short phones.** After "Start the check" on a 360 × 800 phone the lit close-up and the four answers fit together (page 1,104 px, figure 361–705, answers end 897). On a 640 px-tall phone the top of the close-up will be off screen behind the sticky tab bar. Scroll the figure to the top when a question appears.
- **C5. Quietest highlights in the light theme:** saddle and position markers. Visible, but a firmer casing would help on a dim classroom screen. This is inside the approved art, so it is Justin's call.
- **C6. Teacher mode button is the first thing a new student sees** under the title. It is needed there for teachers, but it is the one thing still competing with step 1. Consider moving it to the foot of the page during First Steps.
- **C7. Importing a half-way First Steps backup reports "0 levels set, 0 journal entries…"** and lands on Record with no mention of First Steps. Add "First Steps: 3 of 7" to the count line.

Observed and fine, for the record: an empty 0.9 backup imported on a first visit leaves the student in First Steps (correct, there is no progress in it). Lesson pages are long on a phone (step 1 is 2,584 px, step 2 is 2,264 px at 360); the check and Next are at the bottom.

## Not tested

- What things sound like: frequencies, counts and timings were measured, nobody listened. In the step 7 band one oscillator starts at 440 Hz every two beats; if that is a pitched A rather than a drum voice it would clash with E minor, so it is worth a listen.
- Screen reader behaviour and full keyboard-only use (focus moves were not audited).
- Blocked storage (private mode, sandboxed frame) with First Steps.
- Widths other than 360 and 1280; landscape phones; phones shorter than 800 px.
- The other suites (`test_ui.py`, `test_chords.py`, `test_timing.py`) were not run by me.
- The design checker script (`check_design.py`, spec F17) was not run; look was judged by eye from screenshots.
- Musical facts beyond what the steps state on screen were checked from my own knowledge, not against the class booklet itself, which I was not given.
- Chromium internals behind S4 cause A (the mechanism is inferred from counts and call logs).
